# Informed Consent — Interview for a Diploma Project

**Project:** Identifying emotions from text (sentiment analysis) for the Kazakh and Russian languages
**Researchers:** Nurgaly Ashkenov, Ulan Orazbek (group SE-2406) · **Supervisor:** Kamila Tanyrbergenova

**Purpose.** The interview helps us understand how organisations work with customer and citizen feedback in Kazakh and Russian, so that the requirements of our software are based on real needs.

**What participation involves.** One conversation of 20–40 minutes. You may skip any question and stop at any time without giving a reason.

**How information is used.**
- Notes are anonymised: your name and organisation are replaced by a code (e.g., INT-02, "telecom, support team lead") unless you allow otherwise below.
- Anonymised notes are stored in the project repository and may be quoted in the diploma thesis.
- Audio recordings (if allowed) are used only to check notes, are never published or uploaded to the repository, and are deleted after the project ends.
- Text examples you share are used only after removing personal data and only for this research.
